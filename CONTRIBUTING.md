# Contributing to Awesome Quant Competitions

Thank you for helping keep the quantitative finance competition and fellowship calendar accurate!

## Inclusion Criteria

Every competition, hackathon, datathon, benchmark, or fellowship must:

1. **Be genuinely quantitative:** materially involve mathematical modeling, algorithmic trading, market making, alpha/signal research, time-series forecasting, or systematic execution. Retail broker deposit promotions, paper-trading sweepstakes, and referral-only schemes are excluded.
2. **Link to an official HTTPS primary source:** organizer rules, university quant club site, or official registration portal (no third-party aggregators).
3. **Provide verifiable dates and terms:** clear entry deadline (or explicit recurring cadence), eligibility criteria (e.g., undergraduates, PhD students, open worldwide), and stated prize/funding terms.

## How to Submit or Update a Listing

- **Fastest way:** Open an issue using the [Submit a Quant Competition](https://github.com/KonstantinRogozhkin/awesome-quant-competitions/issues/new?template=submit-competition.yml) form, or submit directly at [quantchallenges.com/submit](https://quantchallenges.com/submit).
- **Pull Requests:** Note that `README.md` is synced automatically every day from [Quant Challenges](https://quantchallenges.com) so deadlines and ATS job counts never go stale. If you want to add or correct a listing permanently, open an Issue so we can verify the official source and publish it into the canonical registry (which propagates back here within 24 hours).

## For University Quant Clubs

University trading societies are welcome to embed or link our auto-updating `.ics` deadline calendar on their club Resources page:

```html
<a href="https://quantchallenges.com/challenges/calendar">2026–2027 Quant Competition Calendar (.ics)</a> — maintained by Quant Challenges
```
